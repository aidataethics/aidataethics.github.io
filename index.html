Measure it by hand, then at scale.

Nine instruments for auditing data and models, used in twelve labs. Each lab takes a quantity you first work out on paper in lecture — a selection rate, an equivalence class, a cosine — and measures it on real data in your browser. Nothing you load or type here leaves your computer.

The labs, in course order

How to use the toolkit

    Your data stays here

    Every tool runs in the browser. Uploaded files are read locally and never sent anywhere. Some labs ask you to hand in a session record; it is off until you turn it on, and you can read every line of it in Settings.

    The data is synthetic, on purpose

    The sample datasets are generated from documented processes, so every disparity you find can be traced to a line of code. The generators are in the project repository.
