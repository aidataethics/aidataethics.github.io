Your timeline

Real headlines from 2012–2020: 288 stories from 36 US outlets, from the Article-Bias-Prediction dataset (Baly et al., 2020). Each is labelled left, center or right by AllSides, which rates mostly by outlet, so the label says where a story was published, not whether its headline is fair. Each action is a signal: read adds 0.5 to the story's topic and side, like 1.0, and repost 1.5, and a repost also reaches your friends, who pass that topic back for a few steps. Everything else fades by 3%. The weights are illustrative: platforms likewise count a repost for more than a like, and a like for more than a click. The link under a headline opens the original article and does not count.

school Learning Point

Filter bubbles occur when recommendation algorithms over-optimize for user engagement, progressively narrowing content diversity. Try the Engagement-optimised mode with high engagement weight to see a strong echo chamber form. Switch to Diversity-aware or click Break the Bubble to see how algorithmic interventions can restore content diversity. The chart tracks 3 diversity metrics: Shannon entropy, Simpson index, and Gini coefficient.
