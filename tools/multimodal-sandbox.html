Multimodal Sandbox

A model that reads images and listens to speech, and answers in text or in a voice. It runs on your own graphics card.

This device cannot run the model

One model, three ways in

LiquidAI’s LFM3-Omni-2B turns a picture into a few hundred tokens, a recording into about twelve tokens a second, and reads both alongside your text. It can answer in writing or in a synthetic voice. Every new way in is a new way to slip it an instruction, and every voice it listens to is one it may hear well or badly.

Images and recordings are turned into numbers in this tab and never leave your computer. The only network traffic is the one-off download of the model from Hugging Face, which your browser then keeps. Record only people who have agreed to it.

    The parts for images and speech download the first time you use them. Needs WebGPU with 16-bit shaders: a recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. On a shared computer, when you finish.

    Word error rate

    Several people read the same sentence. The model transcribes each recording, and each transcript is lined up against the sentence word by word. Every word it got wrong is a substitution, a deletion or an insertion:

    WER = (S + D + I) ÷ N, where N is the number of words in the sentence.

    Before comparing, both are lower-cased and stripped of punctuation. One sentence from a few people is a demonstration, not a study: a real audit needs many sentences per speaker and many speakers per group.
